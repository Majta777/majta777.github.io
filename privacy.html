Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This application is a private tool used to access, manage, analyze, and optimize Amazon Ads advertising data through the Amazon Ads API.

Data Access

The application may access advertising account information made available through the Amazon Ads API, including campaign, ad group, targeting, keyword, search term, performance, placement, and reporting data.

Purpose of Processing

The data is used solely for managing, analyzing, optimizing, and reporting on advertising campaigns associated with Amazon advertising accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner.

Data Sharing

Data obtained through the Amazon Ads API is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Advertising data may be stored locally or on private systems controlled by the application owner for analysis, reporting, backup, and campaign management purposes.

Login with Amazon

Authentication is performed using Login with Amazon. Amazon account passwords are not collected or stored by this application.

Access Revocation

Access to Amazon advertising data may be revoked by removing the application's authorization from the associated Amazon account.
